Mal Crypto · Home screen widget

Your buy zones, on your home screen

See which coins are closest to their next entry without opening anything. It uses the same live prices and buy zones as the watchlist.

  1. Get the free Scriptable app

    It's the app that runs the widget. No account needed.

    Open in the App Store
  2. Copy the widget

    Tap the button below to copy it.

    Copied ✓
  3. Paste it into Scriptable

    Open Scriptable, tap + at the top right, paste, then tap the title at the top and name it Mal Buy Zones. Tap Done.

  4. Add it to your home screen

    Press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Edit then Add Widget, choose Scriptable and pick a size. Then press and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and set Script to Mal Buy Zones.

Small shows the coin closest to an entry, medium shows the top 3 and large shows the top 7. To pin your own coins, set Parameter in Edit Widget to tickers like TRUMP,FIL,SUSHI. Tap the widget to open the full watchlist. iPhone refreshes widgets on its own schedule, usually every few minutes.

  1. Install the Buy Zones app

    Go back to the watchlist in Chrome, tap Get the app and follow the steps. It adds a Buy Zones icon to your home screen that opens straight into the live watchlist.

    Open the watchlist
  2. Live widgets on Android

    Android only allows live home screen widgets from apps installed through the Play Store, so for now the app icon is the quickest way in. The app shows everything the widget would, and more.
